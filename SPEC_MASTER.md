## Product Definition

**One-Sentence Value Proposition:** "We audit your organization's AI usage, score it against a proprietary benchmark database, identify compliance risks under global AI regulations, and sell change management to fix what the audit finds."

**What This Is NOT:** A consulting project. This is a repeatable, scalable intelligence product built on:
1. A proprietary benchmark database that accumulates from every audit
2. A compliance trigger layer spanning 7+ global regulatory frameworks
3. A conversational scoring agent that replaces static intake forms
4. A certified partner network that breaks the founder bottleneck at scale

- **Penalties:** Up to €35M or 7% global revenue

## Five Maturity Dimensions (100-Point Scoring)

Each dimension scores 0-20. Total = 0-100.

### Dimension 1: AWARENESS (0-20)

| Criterion | Points | Source |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Leadership has defined AI strategy or policy | 0-5 | Form Q3.1 / conversation |
| Employees are aware AI tools are available to them | 0-5 | Form Q4.1 / conversation |
| Company has tracked AI tool spend in last 12 months | 0-5 | Form Q2.3 [J] |
| AI is included in team/department planning discussions | 0-5 | Form Q3.2 [J] |

### Dimension 2: ADOPTION (0-20)

| Criterion | Points | Source |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| ≥50% employees actively use ≥1 AI tool weekly (API-verified preferred) | 0-8 | API data / Form Q4.2 |
| ≥3 departments have adopted AI tools (not just IT) | 0-6 | Form Q2.1 / conversation |
| Abandoned tools <30% of trialled tools | 0-6 | Form Q2.2 [J] |

### Dimension 3: INTEGRATION (0-20)

| Criterion | Points | Source |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| AI tools integrated into core workflows (not siloed) | 0-8 | Form Q3.3 [J] |
| AI outputs feed into downstream processes automatically | 0-7 | Form Q3.4 [J] |
| Data flows between AI tools and existing systems | 0-5 | Form Q3.5 |

### Dimension 4: GOVERNANCE (0-20)

| Criterion | Points | Framework |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| AI usage policy exists and communicated to employees | 0-3 | Internal governance |
| GDPR/data privacy implications documented for AI tools | 0-3 | GDPR |
| EU AI Act risk assessment completed (if in scope) | 0-4 | EU AI Act |
| India DPDP compliance for AI processing Indian personal data | 0-3 | DPDP Act 2023 |
| AI risk management framework in use (NIST, ISO 42001, or equivalent) | 0-4 | ISO 42001 / NIST AI RMF |
| Ethical AI review for high-stakes use cases | 0-3 | Ethical AI |

**Scoring note:** If a client is not in scope for a specific framework (e.g., no EU operations, no India data), award full points for that criterion and note "out of scope."

### Dimension 5: ROI REALIZATION (0-20)

| Criterion | Points | Source |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| Company can quantify time saved per tool (hours/week) | 0-7 | Form Q2.4 / conversation |
| AI investment tracked against measurable outcome | 0-7 | Form Q5.1 [J] |
| Cost per AI tool justified by output (rationalization possible) | 0-6 | Form Q2.5 [J] |

### Dimension 6: ETHICAL AI (0-20) [Conditional]

**Activate for:** healthcare, financial services, HR tech, education, retail (recommendation engines), or any company with >500 employees using AI in people-facing decisions.

| Criterion | Points | What to Ask |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| AI outputs regularly tested for demographic bias | 0-5 | "Have you tested whether your AI produces different outcomes across demographic groups?" |
| Users affected by AI decisions are informed | 0-4 | "When AI makes a decision affecting a customer/employee, are they told? Can they appeal?" |
| High-stakes AI decisions have human review | 0-5 | "Is there always a human in the loop before high-stakes AI decisions are acted on?" |
| Protected characteristics not used in AI decisions | 0-3 | "Are your AI tools using gender, race, age, or health data in decisioning?" |
| AI decisions can be explained to affected parties | 0-3 | "Can you explain to a customer how an AI decision about them was reached?" |

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|

| Attribute | Value |

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Status:** | Active |
| **AI-Specific Obligations:** | Automated decision-making rights (Article 22), data minimisation for AI training datasets, Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for high-risk AI processing, special category data cannot be used without explicit consent |

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|

## The 10 Workflows

### WF-01: Client Intake & Qualification

**Trigger:** Prospect replies to outreach OR inbounds

**ICP Criteria:** Headcount 50-500, ≥3 AI tools, budget signal present

**State Machine:**
```
lead → qualified → form_sent
              → stalled (no response after 5 days)
              → rejected (fail ICP)
```

**Timeout Rules:**
- No response to qualification after 5 days → mark stalled, follow-up in 7 days
- No response after second follow-up → mark cold, re-approach in 30 days

---

### WF-02: Audit Intake

**Primary Method:** Conversational Scoring Agent (Claude-powered)
**Fallback Method:** Google Form (for clients who prefer async)

**Google Form Sections:**
1. Company context (name, industry, headcount, countries of operation)
2. Current AI tool stack (tools, departments, spend, abandoned tools)
3. Workflow & process (top 5 repetitive tasks, bottlenecks)
4. People & adoption (% using AI, training status, resistance)
5. Compliance context (countries, high-risk use cases, policies)
6. Goals (what success looks like, biggest concerns)

**Completeness Gate:** If >30% of mandatory fields missing after one chase → proceed with available data, flag all gaps in report

---

### WF-03: Data Collection & Chase

**Trigger:** Incomplete form submission or vague conversational answers

**Actions:**
1. Identify specific gaps — create numbered list
2. Email client requesting specific missing items
3. Timeout: 3 business days for response
4. If no response: proceed with available data, mark all gaps as "data not provided — findings may be incomplete"
5. If >30% missing AND data is from high-risk sector: escalate to client sponsor

---

### WF-04: Audit Analysis & Scoring — Six Modules (Sequential)

**Module 1 — AI Maturity Scoring**
- Input: Tool stack, adoption %, training status, department coverage
- Output: Score 0-100 across 5-6 dimensions
- Failure mode: Insufficient tool data → score with available data, flag gaps explicitly

**Module 2 — Gap Analysis**
- Input: Current tool stack + reported bottlenecks
- Output: Prioritised list of unautomated high-value workflows ranked by (estimated time saved × feasibility)
- Failure mode: Vague bottleneck descriptions → flag for human review, provide best-estimate ranking

**Module 3 — Tool Evaluation**
- Input: List of tools in use + abandoned tools
- Output: Per-tool assessment: fit score, redundancy flags, underutilisation flags, cost-per-value estimate
- Failure mode: Unknown tool → search for tool category + use case → classify and proceed

**Module 4 — Compliance Risk Assessment**
- Input: Countries of operation + AI use cases
- Process: Apply compliance trigger map to identify which frameworks apply
- Output: Per-framework risk status (in-scope/out-of-scope/high-risk/monitoring), specific obligations triggered, recommended actions
- **Mandatory:** Always check EU AI Act high-risk use case list, always check GDPR AI dimension, always check DPDP if India operations present
- **Flag immediately:** CV screening, credit scoring, healthcare AI, employee monitoring AI

**Module 5 — People & Adoption Risk**
- Input: Adoption %, resistance blockers, training status
- Output: Adoption risk score + behavioral friction map (which teams, which barriers, which nudge interventions)
- Barriers taxonomy: Awareness gap / Trust deficit / Skill gap / Workflow mismatch / Leadership signal failure
- For each barrier: map to a specific intervention

**Module 6 — Recommendation Engine**
- Input: All six module outputs
- Output: Prioritised 90-day action plan structured as:
  - Quick wins (0-30 days): Low effort, high impact, no new tool purchases required
  - Medium-term (30-60 days): Moderate effort, requires training or process change
  - Strategic (60-90 days): High effort, potentially requires new tools or structural change
- If contradictory signals across modules: surface the contradiction explicitly, present two scenarios

**Benchmark Comparison:** After all six modules, compare client's overall maturity score against industry sector average

---

### WF-05: Report Generation

**Two Report Formats:**

**Executive Summary (2 pages):**
- Overall maturity score (large visual, 0-100) with dimension breakdown
- Compliance risk summary — which frameworks apply, what's at risk
- Top 3 gaps (prioritised)
- Top 3 quick-win recommendations
- Benchmark position vs sector average
- Next step CTA (debrief call booking link)

**Full Audit Report (8-12 pages):**
- Company profile & audit scope
- AI maturity scorecard — all dimensions with criterion-level detail
- Compliance risk assessment — all applicable frameworks with specific obligations
- Current tool stack assessment — per-tool findings
- Gap analysis — ranked opportunities with effort/impact estimates
- People & adoption risk map — barrier taxonomy + recommended interventions
- 90-day action plan — categorised by timeline and effort
- Benchmark comparison (anonymised peer comparison where available)
- Methodology note — data sources, scoring rubric version, data gaps

**Quality Gate:** Founder must review before delivery. Check for:
- Accuracy of compliance obligations (do not overstate risk)
- Appropriate caveats where data was partial
- Consistency between scorecard and recommendations
- No hallucinated tool assessments or compliance obligations

---

### WF-06: Report Delivery & Debrief

**Trigger:** Report approved in WF-05

**Actions:**
1. Email report (Executive Summary attached, Full Report linked) with scheduling link for debrief
2. Deadline: 5 business days to book debrief
3. Debrief call: 45 minutes
   - Walk through findings (20 min)
   - Listen for resonance — which gap hurts most? (10 min)
   - Introduce change management offer (10 min)
   - Book follow-up (5 min)
4. No debrief booked after 8 days → send one "did anything resonate?" email → wait 3 days → mark ghosted

**Ghosted Recovery:** Re-approach in 30 days with a specific finding ("Given what we found about your CV screening AI and the EU AI Act deadline, I wanted to check in...")

---

### WF-07: Upsell — Change Management Engagement

**Trigger:** Debrief call shows client interest in acting on recommendations

**Proposal Structure (send within 24h of debrief):**
- Scoped to client's top 3 gaps
- Three deliverables: AI training program design, adoption nudge system, 90-day implementation support
- Priced separately from audit
- Timeline and milestones explicit

**Follow-up:** One chase at day 5 if no response. If no response by day 10 → mark closed_lost_upsell, log objection reason.

---

### WF-08: Payment & Contract

**Contract:** Standard service agreement — send via DocuSign or PDF
**Payment Terms:** 50% upfront, 50% on report delivery
**Processors:** Stripe (global), Razorpay (India-based clients)
**Timeout:** Contract due in 3 business days, payment due net-7

---

### WF-09: Repeat Audit & Benchmark Tracking

**Trigger:** 90 days after audit delivery OR after change management engagement completes

**Key Design Questions (resolve before first audit hits 90-day mark):**
1. Is the repeat audit discounted for existing clients? (Recommended: 30% discount)
2. What data carries over from the first audit vs what is re-collected?
3. How is progress tracked against the first audit's baseline score?
4. Does a repeat audit add to the benchmark database as a new row or an update?

---

### WF-10: Client Offboarding

**Trigger:** Client churns after audit or change management engagement

**Minimum Actions:**
1. Exit survey (3 questions max — what worked, what didn't, would you refer)
2. Anonymise and add final maturity data to benchmark database
3. Schedule re-outreach in 6 months ("would you like to see how you've progressed?")
4. Document churn reason for product improvement
