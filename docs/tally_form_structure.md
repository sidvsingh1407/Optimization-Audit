## Form Logic (Conditional Fields)

**If Q11 = "e) None":**
- Skip to Section 7 (note: no tools in use)
- Show message: "We notice you're not currently using AI tools. Your audit will focus on AI readiness assessment."

**If Q18 = "e) Unaware of EU AI Act":**
- Show info box: "The EU AI Act is a new regulation effective August 2026. Non-compliance can result in significant penalties. Your report will include an overview."
